DRAWING STRUCTURE
Build readable forms before fine detail. Keep intended structural contours and joins continuous through visible sections, respecting occlusion and natural curves. Organize hair into coherent root-to-tip locks with sparse internal strands; avoid tangled crossings and floating fragments. Draw furniture edges and supports with clear attachment points. Keep existing lace motifs connected and readable without adding ornament. Let lighting interrupt highlights, not the underlying structure. Simplify uncertain microdetail rather than inventing extra lines. Preserve the reference's line character, edge softness and painterly variation.
