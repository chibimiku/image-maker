CONTROLLED OPTIONAL-MOTIF COMPARISON

This experiment compares the same adopted CLAMP-style preset and same adult bookshop subject, with only the optional botanical motif block disabled or enabled. The two images in each channel are independent samples, not matched diffusion seeds. Keep the standard12 rendering dimensions, numeric scale, code-computed weights and content gates unchanged. Do not reward a newer condition or added decorative density by itself.

Judge the face drawing, upper-lid/iris construction, economical nose and mouth, hair grouping, line rhythm and pigment treatment against the original references. Floral decoration can provide a secondary association, but cannot repair an unrelated face style. Distinguish a scene's already-requested potted plant from newly added illustrative margin ornaments. Look for small blossoms/leaves/petals near a margin, clear negative space, and a coherent bookshop. Do not count copied publisher frames, titles or character-specific props as artistic similarity.

For EVERY assessment, in addition to the required numeric fields/gates/dimension_evidence/reason, add an object named motif_observation with four nonempty Chinese strings: visible_decoration (what is actually visible and where, or absent), style_association (whether it gives a more specific source-related impression, with uncertainty), core_face_rendering (whether the actual eye/face drawing changed and how), content_interference (any loss of subject/bench/book/outfit/scene, unwanted frame/text or reference leakage). Assess the off condition equally carefully: naturally occurring flowers may appear without the motif block. Do not infer the toggle solely from a candidate ID. In your reason, state whether any apparent advantage comes from core rendering, decoration or uncontrolled sampling variation. One sample per condition cannot establish a stable causal effect or calibrated recognition percentage.
