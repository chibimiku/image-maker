# 固定色板条款模板（知识编译版）

用途：`prompts/color-knowledge/fixed-palette-v1.json` 的 `knowledge` 版本条款来源。

知识编译版写**同一套颜色、同一区域分配、同一保护范围**，只增加明度/彩度层级与色彩关系说明；
不额外改变物件、构图、光照时间或画法，也不删掉简洁版的关键约束（见 `fixed-palette-v1-simple.md`
的撰写规则 1~5）。来源页在 `prompts/color-knowledge/` 的配色知识库与
`docs/261003-color-improve/` 的《256色配色图典》转录中核过，只借用关系方法，不搬运案例的内容、
角色、服装或面积数字。

---

## P1 冷蓝 + 红

- Establish a cool blue base as the dominant colour area: the river, sky, banks, distant foliage and the shadow side of the subject use cool blue and blue-grey of low saturation, with value variation across the distance so the scene keeps depth instead of a flat wash.
- Keep the near blue slightly darker and the distant blue lighter and desaturated; this value ladder carries the depth.
- Reserve the red as the accent of highest chroma, small in area, placed only on the existing waistband and the existing ribbon bows on the shoes; the accent stays red, not pink.
- Let the accent keep its chroma while the surrounding blue stays muted, so the accent reads by chroma contrast rather than by size; keep it spatially concentrated on those two regions.
- Use the cool shadows as part of the blue area, not as a grey veil, and keep overall daylight brightness.
- Keep the subject's own colours: natural skin, ivory-white dress and dark neutral platform shoes.
- Do not add red objects, a second person, or change the time of day.
