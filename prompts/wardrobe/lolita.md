Japanese Lolita fashion: choose a coherent sweet, classical or gothic direction suited to requested colours. Design an OP dress or JSK with a compatible blouse; establish a structured waist, layered skirt volume and plausible seams. A near-knee bell or A-line skirt is a default, not a universal requirement.

Organize lace, ruffles, pintucks, bows and optional prints along collars, cuffs, bodice and hem; connect trims to garment panels, leaving calm fabric areas. Coordinate visible optional headwear, socks/tights and shoes without replacing held objects. Respect gravity, crop and occlusion; do not expose hidden support garments. Honour explicit clothing colours; otherwise coordinate a palette. Dark Gothic colours are allowed.

Do not substitute a generic bow-decorated dress, maid uniform, lingerie or idol costume. Do not force pink, a youthful face, a tiny body, a tea-party scene or the reference's painting medium.
