Japanese Lolita fashion is a clothing design, independent of painting style. Build a connected bodice or blouse, a plausible waist transition and a deliberately expanded bell or A-line skirt with coordinated trims. Fullness belongs to clothing, not a changed body. OP dresses, JSK with blouse, or a coordinated blouse-and-skirt set are alternatives; cultural hybrids use their own upper construction. Apply only features visible in the requested crop. Do not substitute lingerie, a maid uniform, an idol costume, a straight robe or a generic dress with a single bow. Optional colours and ornaments are not mandatory. Keep explicitly protected colours, accessories and held objects in their requested roles and positions.
