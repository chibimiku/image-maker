GLOBAL COLOUR HIERARCHY: Render every material as a continuous, readable base colour with a few broad shadow and reflected-light passages that follow its form. Small painted texture, lace and grain may sit on top, but must not break the underlying colour mass into unrelated bright flecks. Give the scene one clear primary light relationship; reserve the highest-value sparkles for a small number of intentional focal motifs and keep ordinary surface glints quieter. Preserve the requested palette, luminous fantasy atmosphere, ornate details and soft layered brushwork. Avoid scattered equal-brightness cyan/white highlights and glossy 3D specular lighting across every surface.
