LIMITED PALETTE EXPERIMENT: Make a deliberate whole-scene reduction in perceptual colour variety. Use roughly eight to ten dominant hue families for the complete illustration, reusing them across related materials rather than creating a new tint for every small mark. Within each material, organise colour into a base, one shadow family and one highlight family. Keep the character's specified intrinsic hair, eye, skin and clothing colours, the requested cool environment and all intentional motifs. Reduce scattered cyan/white sparkle and pearlescent 3D-like reflections so that detail is carried mainly by shape and line. Preserve ornate lace, layered fabric, soft painterly depth and the scene's identity; do not turn it into hard cel shading or a poster.
