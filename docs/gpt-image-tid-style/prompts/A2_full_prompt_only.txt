Polished semi‑realistic cinematic anime: elegant, flowing compositions. Color: luminous saturated midtones, neon focal accents; warm/cool balance (teal OK); preserve highlights, soft modeled shadows, no crushed blacks, occasional pearlescent shifts. Lighting: multi‑source (key+rim+fill), colored rim, gentle color‑bleed, soft volumetrics + many micro‑speculars; AO with selective crisp planar shadows. Water/reflection: multi‑layer inversion/refraction/ripple passes; ripples = crisp specular rims + bloom + displacement; droplets/bubbles refractive with internal reflections, catch‑lights and slight chromatic separation. Edges/linework: painterly edge‑first, near‑absent lines; vanishingly thin color‑matched contours only as accents. Form: idealized anime proportions, soft SSS, restrained expression. Eyes: layered irises, radial gradients, inner glows, multiple catchlights. Hair: ribbon groupings, broad gradients with sharp specular bands and fine strand strokes. Materials: smooth low‑texture skin, soft fabrics with micro‑shadowing, crisper folds on structured garments, satins/metal trims with clustered micro‑glints, translucent pearlescent shifts, glass/wet with crisp speculars, refraction cues and slight chromatic fringing. Overlays: clustered particles, motif bokeh, near‑field orbs as occluders optional. Rendering/finish: hybrid painterly block→refine→speculars, additive glow layers, subtle chromatic aberration/prismatic flares optional, dense micro‑glints on trims/wet surfaces, selective focal sharpening, soft periphery. Rules: avoid flat cel‑shading as sole method; keep linework extremely restrained; maintain pristine high‑fidelity; underwater/wet contexts require layered caustics, bubble refraction and clustered micro‑speculars.
